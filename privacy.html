Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chromiva (the “App”), developed by AppSweat (“we”, “us”), handles information when you use AI personal color analysis, AI try-on, AI hair colour and the on-device styling tools.

Short version: A photo you choose is sent securely only for the AI feature you request. Our AI provider, OpenAI, may keep it and the result for up to 30 days to run the service and detect abuse, and does not use it to train its models. Outfit Color and Makeup run on your iPhone. Chromiva does not require your name or email; purchases are associated with an anonymous app identifier.

1. Information the App Processes

  • Portraits for color analysis: a photo you take or select is uploaded to our Firebase-hosted cloud function and passed to our AI service provider to produce your requested color analysis.
  • Photos for AI try-on: your photo and a photo of the clothing you want to try are uploaded the same way to create a try-on image.
  • Photos for AI hair colour: a crop of your photo around your head and hair is uploaded the same way to create a hair colour image. Only the recoloured hair is kept from the result; the rest of your photo is not changed.
  • On-device features: Outfit Color, Color Drape and Makeup process your photo on your iPhone. These photos are not uploaded for these features.
  • Analysis results: generated season, palette, undertone, contrast, and styling guidance. Results and a thumbnail are saved locally for History and, when iCloud is available for Chromiva, synchronized to the user's private CloudKit database.
  • AI images: generated try-on and hair colour images, with a smaller copy of the photo you used and the chosen shade, are saved locally in History and, when iCloud is available for Chromiva, synchronized to the user's private CloudKit database.
  • Preferences: language, appearance, self-declared style and color preferences, and favourite and custom colours are stored locally. Supported preferences may also synchronize through Apple's iCloud key-value storage.
  • Anonymous identifiers: Firebase creates an anonymous user identifier used to secure requests and associate usage with the correct Color Pass balance.
  • Purchase data: RevenueCat and Apple process product, transaction, entitlement, and Color Pass information. We do not receive your full payment card details.
  • Technical data: Firebase App Check may process device attestation information to prevent unauthorized API use.

2. How We Use Information

  • Provide the color analysis, AI try-on and AI hair colour images you request.
  • Maintain and spend Color Pass balances.
  • Secure the service, prevent abuse, diagnose failures, and provide support.
  • Display your saved analysis history on your device.

3. AI Processing

A photo is sent only when you start an AI feature yourself: Start Analysis for color analysis, Try it on for AI try-on, or Try this colour for AI hair colour. It is processed by our cloud function and by our AI provider, OpenAI, to generate the result.

Retention by our AI provider: under OpenAI's API data policies, the photos and the results sent through its API may be retained for up to 30 days to provide the service and to identify abuse, and are then removed unless OpenAI is legally required to keep them longer. OpenAI does not use API inputs or outputs to train its models unless we opt in, and we have not opted in. Our cloud function does not save your photos or generated images.

Makeup and Google MediaPipe: the Makeup tool finds facial landmarks with Google's MediaPipe library on your iPhone; your photo is not sent to Google. MediaPipe does, however, send Google metrics about how the library is used and performs: SDK usage, installs and session counts; inference counts and hardware performance; the app's identifier and general characteristics of the processed media (for example, that it is an image); and the operating system and its version. Google uses these metrics to measure and improve MediaPipe and to detect misuse, under the Google Privacy Policy and the MediaPipe terms. This happens when the Makeup tool or the makeup preview on a result screen runs.

Do not upload a photo unless you have the right and permission to use it. Chromiva is designed for portraits of the user and is not intended for identifying people or inferring health, ethnicity, or other sensitive traits.

4. Service Providers

We use service providers to operate Chromiva, including Google Firebase for authentication, App Check, cloud functions, and operational data; OpenAI for AI color analysis, AI try-on and AI hair colour; RevenueCat for purchases and virtual currency; Google MediaPipe, an on-device library that finds facial landmarks for Makeup (see section 3); and Apple for App Store distribution, payment processing, and optional private iCloud/CloudKit synchronization. Their processing is governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

5. Local Data, iCloud and Retention

Chromiva keeps a local replica of your History — color analyses, AI try-ons and AI hair colours — so the App remains usable when offline. If the device is signed in to iCloud and iCloud access is available to Chromiva, Apple CloudKit synchronizes existing and future History records, result data, generated images and photo copies through the user's private database. This private data belongs to the user's iCloud account, counts toward the user's iCloud storage quota, and can be restored on devices using the same account. App preferences may synchronize through iCloud key-value storage. If iCloud is unavailable, Chromiva continues using local storage and resumes synchronization when possible.

Deleting a History item removes it from the local store and the deletion is synchronized to CloudKit when available. Uninstalling Chromiva removes its local data but does not by itself guarantee immediate deletion of a previously synchronized iCloud copy. Cloud operational records associated with an anonymous identifier may separately include request status and, for color analysis, the generated text result; they do not include your photos or generated images. We retain information only as reasonably necessary to operate, secure, and support the service.

6. Your Choices

  • You may deny Camera or Photo Library access and choose not to run an analysis.
  • You may delete saved analyses, try-ons and hair colours from History, or delete all of Chromiva's synced data in Settings › iCloud Sync; synchronized deletions are propagated to CloudKit when available.
  • You can manage iCloud availability for apps through your device's Apple Account and iCloud settings.
  • You may delete the App to remove its local data. Previously synchronized private iCloud data may remain associated with your iCloud account until removed through the App or Apple's iCloud data-management controls.
  • You may contact us to request help with cloud operational data associated with your anonymous account. We may need diagnostic information from your device to locate it.

7. Security

We use authenticated requests, Firebase App Check, encrypted network transport, and server-side purchase verification safeguards. No system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Children

Chromiva is not directed to children under 13 or the minimum digital-consent age in their region. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes

We may update this policy as the App changes. The current version will remain available on this page with its updated date.
