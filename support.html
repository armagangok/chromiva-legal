Frequently Asked Questions

What makes a good analysis photo?

Use a clear, unfiltered portrait in even natural light. Face the camera directly, keep your full face visible, and avoid strong colored lighting or deep shadows.

Why can results vary?

Lighting, camera processing, makeup, filters, and surrounding colors can change how skin, hair, and eyes appear. Try another well-lit photo if a result feels inaccurate.

How do Color Passes work?

Color Passes are consumable one-time purchases. One Pass is used after one analysis completes successfully. Your current balance appears in Settings and on the purchase screen.

My balance looks outdated. What should I do?

Open Settings while connected to the internet. Chromiva refreshes the current RevenueCat balance when Settings or the purchase screen opens. You can also use Restore Purchases for purchase-recovery troubleshooting.

Where are my results saved?

Your History and photo thumbnails are stored locally on your device. Deleting a History item removes that saved local result. Uninstalling the App removes local history.

Can Chromiva diagnose skin or health conditions?

No. Chromiva provides creative styling guidance only and does not provide medical or dermatological assessments.

A purchase failed or I need a refund.

Purchases and refunds are handled by Apple. Check your App Store purchase history or use Apple’s official refund request process. Include any Apple transaction details when contacting support, but never send payment card information.
